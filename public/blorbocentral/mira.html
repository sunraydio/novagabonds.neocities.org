Mira is one of the main characters of in-development comic series Fruiperheroes, wherein they get to stare into the future & the past & ask the questions of what makes someone unforgivable and how far love can stretch before it becomes a ghost of what it once was.


character synopsis aside they're also among one of my favorite characters & one I've had since ~2017 or 2018 or something like that, so they've been someone I've taken with me from childhood into adulthood (even if they, for the most part, have remained the same in time). something about how we change and grow and find our people and sometimes our people change and grow and find new people and that is just part of life. but sometimes we try to cling to each other anyway.
