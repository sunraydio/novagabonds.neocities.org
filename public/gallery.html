The Forge

This is a project that continues to be near and dear to my heart. I've had these characters since I was nine, & now they've grown into a comic/visual novel/animatic??? concept that I'm still developing visually & deciding where they want to be! It's hard to contain characters to one single mode of storytelling.

Fruiperheroes

Fruiperheroes is a comic I've been making with Mpekamitzii since the big 2017/2018 era! There is of course so much more info about it and connected sites/users in its own tab on my website but the basic premise is teenage superheroes, fake Australia, summer shenanigans forever, and grief & love intertwined.

Keeper of the Lost Cities

Back in 2020, this was a defining fandom of my art career! I spent plenty of time creating worlds within worlds, taking kind of standard fantasy worldbuilding and making new things with my friends that felt like a representation of who we were at the time. I also think it's important to note I had so much love in my heart at the time and most of it was going into this, so that is the rose tinge of everything you'll see in many places.
